## Objective

Rambak Siswanto's website is intended as a real UMKM company profile, trust builder, partnership entry point, light product showcase, marketplace discovery route, and WhatsApp discussion channel. It is not a direct checkout, CRM, or order database.

Priority audiences are prospective partners, suppliers, distributors, souvenir shops, resellers, retail customers, public institutions, and academic stakeholders. The desired character is calm, mature, authentic, warm, culturally rooted, and modern without fabricated heritage.
