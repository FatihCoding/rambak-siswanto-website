# Rambak Owner Validation

## Trigger

Use when preparing an owner interview, reviewing owner feedback, registering permission, approving public content, or reconciling products and authentic photos.

Do not use to infer approval from silence, a marketplace URL, a photo label, or general trust.

## Inputs

- Current preview and owner-review checklist
- Business facts register, product matrix, and photo manifest
- Traceable interview notes, recordings, primary documents, or written owner decisions

## Workflow

1. Present the current draft, recommendation, alternative, risk, and exact question.
2. Record who decided, when, the scope, and the evidence reference.
3. Separate ownership from permission to publish.
4. Mark each item approve, revise, or withhold.
5. Resolve conflicts through owner confirmation or primary evidence.
6. Keep private source material outside the public repository.
7. Update all affected registers and public copy only after the decision is traceable.
8. Return a final preview for sign-off.

## Outputs

- Completed validation record
- Evidence references without private source exposure
- Publication decisions and required corrections

## Stop and escalate

Stop when identity spelling conflicts, evidence is ambiguous, consent scope is missing, a photo subject has not consented, legal validity is uncertain, or the owner requests withdrawal. Formal legal enforceability must be reviewed by a qualified Indonesian legal professional.
